Service List 1.2 (NGSL)**, English definitions from the NGSL workbook supplied
for this project, and concise Chinese glosses derived from **ECDICT**.

- Project: <https://www.newgeneralservicelist.com/>

- Definitions: NGSL 1.2 workbook (`NGSL_1.2_with_English_definitions.xlsx`)
